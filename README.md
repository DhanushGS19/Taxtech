> *"Next-Gen UPI Payments App with Locked Expense Vault & Integrated Personal AI Chartered Accountant."*

## ⚡ Overview & Concept

**TechPay + TaxSaathi** combines two powerful financial systems into a unified platform:

1. **Face 1 (Dominant Face): TechPay – Google Pay Style UPI & Payments Hub**  
   - Modern, intuitive fintech interface for making instant UPI payments via QR code, phone number, or UPI ID.
   - **Payment History with Real-World Location History**: Captures where every payment was made (e.g. Starbucks Indiranagar Bengaluru, Apollo Pharmacy Sector 18 Noida, D-Mart Hinjewadi Pune) with full street addresses, landmarks, and GPS coordinates.
   - **Fixed Budget System & Locked Expense Vault**: Locks a fixed portion of monthly income for mandatory obligations (Home Loan EMI, House Rent, School Tuition Fees, Utility Bills, Health Insurance).
   - **Active Overspending Guardrails**: Reminds and alerts users whenever non-essential spending risks their locked bill/EMI buffer.
   - **AutoPay (NPCI e-Mandates)**: Automated recurring debits for SIPs, electricity, and subscriptions with pause/resume controls.
   - **Wealth & Investments Hub**: Buy and sell 24K 999.9 pure digital gold in real time (minimum ₹10) with live bullion tickers, plus ELSS Tax Saver SIPs.

2. **Face 2 (Second Face): TaxSaathi – Personal AI Chartered Accountant**  
   - Acts as a virtual Personal Chartered Accountant analyzing financial transactions, identifying taxable income, tracking deductions, and providing personalized tax advice.
   - **Three Intelligent Engines Architecture**:
     - *Transaction Intelligence Engine*: Automatically identifies transaction categories and tax sections (Sec 15, Sec 16(ia), Sec 44ADA, Sec 80C, Sec 80D, Sec 24(b), Sec 56(2)(x)).
     - *Verified Indian Tax Rules Engine*: Computes exact liabilities under both New Regime (Budget 2024 revised slabs) and Old Regime with 87A rebate and 4% cess.
     - *AI Personal CA Engine ("CA Vikram Saathi")*: Guides users via voice and chat in both English and Hinglish (हिंदी).
   - **Document Intelligence**: Upload and extract Bank statements (CSV/PDF), Form 16 Part B, and AIS/TIS JSON.
   - **Compliance Manager**: Personal tax calendar tracking Advance Tax quarterly installments (15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Dec, 15 Mar) and ITR return deadlines.
   - **Tax Health Score & Audit Center**: 0-100 score analyzing record completeness, cash deposit limits (>₹10 Lakhs SFT), and duplicate detection.
   - **Official ITR Ready Computation**: Printable/exportable CA-stamped computation sheet.
